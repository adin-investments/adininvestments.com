01 — What we do

Every stage of a property’s life.

From the first offer to the final sale, Adin works on its own account, with its own capital and judgment.

Acquisitions

Houses, land, small multifamily and small commercial property, including properties that need work or don’t fit a conventional sale.

Development

Land and new construction, taken from feasibility and permitting through the build and the sale.

Renovation & Repositioning

Properties that need capital, attention or a better plan, renovated and returned to the market.

Joint Ventures

Partnerships with landowners and builders when combining land and experience makes a project work.

03 — About

Two decades in Western Washington real estate.

Founded in 2005, Adin Investments, Inc. invests in, develops and renovates residential and commercial real estate across Western Washington.

The company’s principal, Clint Aguon, has led its investment, development and renovation work for more than twenty years.
